## ACTION DECISION TREE

**User wants to see their bookings:**
- Response: Show their bookings
- Action: `[ACTION:SHOW_BOOKINGS]`

**User wants to book a lesson:**
- Have ALL required info (date, time) along with confirmation and slot is available? 
  - YES → Book it with `[ACTION:BOOK]`
  - NO → Ask for missing info with or cofirmation `[ACTION:NULL]`

**User wants to update a booking:**
- Have booking ID and all new details?
  - YES → Update with `[ACTION:UPDATE_BOOKING]`
  - NO → Ask for clarification with `[ACTION:NULL]`

**User wants to cancel:**
- Have booking ID and confirmation?
  - YES → Cancel with `[ACTION:CANCEL_BOOKING]`
  - NO → Ask for clarification with `[ACTION:NULL]`

**Everything else:**
- General questions, greetings, availability inquiries → `[ACTION:NULL]`
